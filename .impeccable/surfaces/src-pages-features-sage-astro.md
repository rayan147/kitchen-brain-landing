# Sage specialist feature surface brief

## Thesis

Sage is an available working assistant, not a promise or a generic chat box. A chef-owner can ask a kitchen question during setup or during the working week, inspect the records behind the answer, and approve or discard the only work Sage can prepare.

## Audience and job

The primary reader is an independent caterer or chef-owner who wants a faster way to find what needs attention without handing judgment or kitchen records to an opaque assistant.

## Visual world

Stay inside CostCook's Kitchen Ticket system: warm paper, deep ink, working green, restrained amber, serif editorial statements, square record frames and fine rules. The distinctive motif is a question traveling into a visible evidence trail. Authentic CostCook captures carry the product proof; generated imagery is concept-only and must not ship as evidence.

## First viewport

Use an asymmetrical editorial split. The statement and actions sit at left with an explicit Available now status. At right, the authentic Sage answer capture is a large evidence sheet, while a smaller setup capture shows the Ask Sage entry point feeding into the same workspace.

## Information architecture

Use one specialist route. Lead with availability and the answer/source contract, then show a short product video, explain the setup hand-off, present the six supported kitchen questions, show one sourced answer, explain guardrails and recovery, answer six practical FAQs, and close with a start/demo choice.

## Workflow decision

Marketing evaluation is low-frequency and high-information. Using Sage is frequent enough to keep the question, answer and source links in one frame; approving prepared work is higher risk and remains a separate explicit review. Use a hybrid page shape: denormalized proof and questions, normalized commitment and guardrail explanation. No broader route-family restructuring is needed.

## Video direction

The video is a concise silent product walkthrough made only from authentic captures. It moves from Ask Sage during setup, to setup-aware questions and a route back, to a sourced answer, then to the human approval boundary. Use persistent open captions, native controls, a static poster, no autoplay, no invented cursor interaction and no fake response generation.

## Claim discipline

Sage is available now by release-owner confirmation on 2026-08-29. Describe only the six implemented tools, setup-aware suggestions and return path, source collection, role checks, limits, kill switch and explicit shopping-list proposal approval. Do not name a model or provider, say Sage learns or remembers the business, promise perfect answers, imply autonomous changes, or add an unsupported seventh tool.

## Mobile behavior

Stack copy before evidence, keep status and actions visible, use the mobile Sage capture, preserve readable video captions, reflow the six questions into a single list, and keep answer/source/approval relationships explicit without horizontal scrolling.

## Finish contract

The route is finished when Sage reads as available across every shared source, onboarding assistance is factual and visible, the dedicated video works in MP4 and WebM, six supported questions and six FAQs are present, controls and disclosures work by keyboard, and the page passes the full five-width route-family browser matrix.
