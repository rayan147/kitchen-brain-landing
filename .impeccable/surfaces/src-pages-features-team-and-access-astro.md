# Team & Access specialist feature surface brief

## Thesis

CostCook has three real roles and a short list of enforced sensitive-action boundaries, not a configurable permission system. Show Owner, Manager and Staff as one escalation path inside a shared kitchen workspace, and state the limits before the reader invites anyone.

## Audience and job

The primary reader is a chef-owner deciding whether managers and kitchen staff can work in CostCook without crossing the account, recipe-lifecycle or higher-risk Sage boundaries the owner needs to retain.

## Visual world

Stay inside CostCook's Kitchen Ticket system: warm paper, cream and soft-green fields, deep ink, restrained amber, serif editorial statements, square access slips, fine rules and green actions. The distinctive motif is a stack of staff passes that resolves into an inspectable role ledger; it must not become a generic persona-card catalogue or a permission matrix.

## First viewport

Use an asymmetrical editorial split. The statement and actions sit at left; three overlapping Owner, Manager and Staff passes sit at right, with the owner pass carrying the sensitive-work hierarchy. Make the page's plain-language promise visible immediately: three roles, a finite set of real boundaries, and no suggestion of custom permissions.

## Information architecture

Use one specialist route. Lead with the three-role contract, explain the shared record and server-side enforcement, present the roles as an escalation ledger, then give the unsupported boundary its own prominent section. Resolve practical invitation and visibility questions in semantic disclosures, close with start/demo actions, and link onward to Sage, Recipes & Costing and the complete feature index. The homepage remains a concise introduction and points readers here for the inspectable boundary list; the shared feature navigation exposes this route as a shipped destination.

## Workflow decision

Evaluating access is low-frequency but high-risk. Keep the complete role relationship and unsupported cases visible in the page flow instead of hiding them behind a configurable-looking control. Owner includes Manager responsibility; Manager carries its named protected work; Staff uses the shared operational workspace without a route around higher-role actions. No route-family restructuring or interactive permission editor is needed.

## Claim discipline

Name only the enforced Owner, Manager and Staff boundaries supported by the shipped feature register. State that new invitations join as Staff, sensitive actions are checked on the server, and CostCook has no custom roles, per-screen hiding, field-level permissions or fine-grained permission grid. Do not imply that roles partition every record or screen.

## Mobile behavior

Stack copy before the role passes, convert the overlapping pass composition into a readable vertical sequence where needed, keep the boundary ledger in Owner-to-Manager-to-Staff order, and preserve the explicit unsupported-case section without horizontal scrolling. Actions and disclosure summaries retain accessible touch targets and keyboard behavior.

## Finish contract

The surface is finished when the homepage gives a concise role introduction, the specialist route makes every supported sensitive-action boundary and every material access limit inspectable, Team & Access is reachable from shared feature navigation, semantic disclosures work without client-side JavaScript, and desktop/mobile captures show a complete reading order with no horizontal overflow.
