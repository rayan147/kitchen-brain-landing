# Invoices & price-list import surface brief

## Thesis

Supplier documents can enter through one queue without becoming the same kind of truth. Present Invoice Import and Price-list Import as distinct chapters joined by a staged review and a human confirmation gate.

## Audience and job

The primary reader is an independent caterer or working chef-owner who needs current ingredient prices for the next quote but cannot let uncertain extraction silently change recipe costs.

## Visual world

Stay inside CostCook's Kitchen Ticket system: warm paper, deep ink, working green, amber caution and commitment signals, serif editorial headlines, fine rules, code-native operational records, and no invented product screenshot. The page's distinctive motif is a receiving-light table: source paper at left, staged rows under inspection, and confirmed records only after the gate.

## First viewport

Use an asymmetrical editorial split. The statement and actions sit at left; a layered invoice and price-list source become a staged review ledger at right. A visible `SOURCE → STAGE → YOU CONFIRM` path must make the safety model legible before individual capabilities are introduced.

## Information architecture

Use one specialist route with anchored Invoice Import and Price-list Import chapters. Show the common intake rule first, then distinguish an invoice as a purchase from a price list as an offer. Keep exception handling and six FAQs in the main reading flow; put the detailed shipped capability inventory in semantic disclosures.

## Workflow decision

This marketing evaluation is low-frequency and high-information; the underlying import is high-risk because it can change financial and recipe-cost data. Use a hybrid page shape: an immediate overview, two visible product chapters, then a staged review and recovery model. Do not turn the marketing route into a functional upload wizard, add tabs, or imply that confirmation is automatic. No broader route-family restructuring is needed.

## Claim discipline

Use only `src/lib/features.ts` and the release claim ledger. Clearly label illustrative values. Do not claim guaranteed OCR accuracy, automatic posting or matching, accounting export, approvals, bill pay, savings, processing speed, or functionality borrowed from Meez or MarginEdge.

## Mobile behavior

Stack copy before evidence, reflow source and staged rows into label-value blocks, preserve source/stage/confirm order, keep document labels and state words visible without color, and prevent page-level or nested horizontal scrolling.

## Finish contract

The page is finished when it distinguishes invoice import from price-list import, shows the original beside staged facts, explains unreadable, unmatched, duplicate, and changed-pack recovery, answers six practical objections, routes the existing feature-menu entry to the specialist page, and passes desktop/mobile verification.
