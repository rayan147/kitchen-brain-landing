# Demo video narration + caption script

Voice: the founder, chef to chef. Warm, plain, first person. Contractions,
short sentences. No marketing words, no em-dashes. Read it like you're
showing a cook the tool on your own laptop, not presenting.

Two ways to produce the voice track (either works with the same file):

1. **Founder-read (preferred, most authentic).** Record each beat as its
   own take, reading the NARRATION lines exactly as written. Quiet room,
   phone mic is fine, hold a beat of silence at each take's start/end.
2. **Neural TTS (ships now, swappable later).** A natural neural voice
   reads the same lines, one audio file per beat, so a founder re-record
   later drops in without re-editing the video.

Every claim below is ground-truthed against the live demo instance
(kitchen-brain-demo.vercel.app, Rodriguez backyard wedding, 200 guests,
captured 2026-07-19). If the sample dataset changes, re-verify the
numbers before re-recording.

Captions are the narration, chunked one readable line at a time (max
~46 chars), burned in and synced. Holds are minimums; footage always
outlasts its narration by at least a second.

chrome. The demo hostname contains the internal product name and must
not appear in frame.
