# Story Tracker — Case price to portion cost

- **Idea:** A cook knows the case price and the plated amount, but trim, pack units, and yield hide the portion cost between them.
- **Character:** Wants the plate cost; needs each conversion visible; remembers buying extra after a yield miss; rounds the difficult units in their head.
- **Plot:** Case arrives → usable quantity shrinks → recipe takes a share → portion takes a share → line cost appears.
- **Scenes:** Read the case; normalize units; apply yield; cost the recipe quantity; divide by portions; check the result.
- **Voice:** Second person; concrete and arithmetic-first; no magic automation language.
- **Dialogue:** A supplier price becomes a cost the plate can actually carry.
- **Headline:** How to cost a recipe from case price to portion cost
- **Snap:** The skins are in the case price, but they are not on the plate.
- **Life:** A wet case lands on the scale and the recipe still says “six onions.”
- **Connection:** Portion cost hands off to per-guest menu cost.
