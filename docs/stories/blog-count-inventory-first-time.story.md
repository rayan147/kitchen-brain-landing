### Step 1 — The Idea

> A caterer whose shopping list keeps buying what's already on the shelf, because CostCook won't trust a count older than 2 days, and who doesn't know where to start counting.

### Step 2 — Your Character

- **Want:** Stop buying a case of something they already have.
- **Need:** One counted shelf, then the next, instead of a whole-kitchen count that never happens.
- **Wound:** A weekend lost to a full stock-take that was out of date by Tuesday.
- **Flaw:** Trusts the estimate on screen because it looks precise.

### Step 8 — Cool Talk (the one snap line)

> "22.5 lb on the estimate, and it still buys a case." (the green-bean row before its count)

### Step 11 — Revise and Finish

- **Kept to the app's words:** the 2-day rule, "Zero records an empty shelf", "Spoilage is not in the estimate", "Count them first to buy less", "This list does not order anything".
- **Removed claims:** who may count; a par topping up the list; how long a count takes; "produce and dairy are perishable" (the flag is per item, so the tip names the window's label instead).
- **Found while shooting:** Build shopping list on Inventory opens a confirmation, not the list page. The list then appears as its own Shopping list button. The steps follow that. The count unit defaults to the ingredient's base unit (kg) even in a US kitchen, so the step says to pick the unit.
- **Final image:** the next shelf to count is the one whose line still says "No recent count".
