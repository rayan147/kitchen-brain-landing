### Step 1 — The Idea

> A caterer looking at a month where they bought thousands more food than their events used, who wants to know how much of it is real loss, and who can't tell stock from waste from a missing invoice.

### Step 2 — Your Character

- **Want:** One food-cost number for last month they can stand behind.
- **Need:** The gap split into what's on the shelf, what was logged, and what is still a question.
- **Wound:** A month-end where the whole difference got called "waste" and the crew took the blame.
- **Flaw:** Logs waste at month end, so it lands in the wrong month.

### Step 8 — Cool Talk (the one snap line)

> "A gap is a question, not a waste entry." (the app's own line on Reconcile food cost)

### Step 11 — Revise and Finish

- **Kept to the app's words:** "Food only", "A gap is a question, not a waste entry", "confirmation freezes their costs", "A sent purchase order does not mean food has been received", "Today's count does not replace a historical count", "New waste entries use today's date and do not change a past month", "No waste is a valid outcome", "neither proves loss", the 14-day count window.
- **Every figure is the sample kitchen's September, checked by hand:** 19,750.41 + 2,553.14 − 12,599.25 − 96.48 − 5,181.11 = 4,426.71; brisket and lamb rows add up; Analytics' 7,054.68 minus Purchases' 4,426.71 is the 2,627.97 shelf growth.
- **Removed claims:** that the gap is waste; what a normal gap is; how long a close takes; corrections and adjustments (real on the feature page, not shot here).
- **Added after the shoot:** a fix entry for Analytics showing a bigger number than Purchases, because a reader will meet both.
- **Final image:** whatever no record explains stays on the page, named.
