# Story Tracker — Food cost per guest

- **Idea:** A caterer needs a price per guest before replying, but revenue and food cost are being judged on different sheets.
- **Character:** Wants a safe quote; needs inspectable working; remembers the job that looked profitable; tends to start from last year's price.
- **Plot:** Price request → separate costs → one per-guest calculation → target check → decision.
- **Scenes:** Define the number; gather inputs; work the example; compare the target; test exceptions; price the next job.
- **Voice:** Second person; calm, exact, kitchen-literate; no hacks, secrets, or guarantees.
- **Dialogue:** A familiar price becomes a decision only after the food has had its say.
- **Headline:** How to calculate food cost per guest for a catering event
- **Snap:** A round price can still make a crooked quote.
- **Life:** The customer is waiting while the latest invoice sits beside the event sheet.
- **Connection:** Cost per guest hands off to menu pricing and quote review.
