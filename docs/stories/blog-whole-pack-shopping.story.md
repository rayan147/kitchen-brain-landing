# Story Tracker — Shopping in whole packs

- **Idea:** A kitchen knows the recipe quantity, but the supplier sells cases, bags, and bottles that do not divide cleanly.
- **Character:** Wants a buy list; needs unit conversion and rounding visible; remembers a short case; writes decimals that cannot be ordered.
- **Plot:** Menu quantity → consolidated ingredient need → pack conversion → whole-pack rounding → supplier list.
- **Scenes:** Start from portions; combine duplicates; convert units; round; account for on-hand facts carefully; place the order.
- **Voice:** Second person; practical and cautious; no automatic inventory promise.
- **Dialogue:** Recipe amounts become quantities a supplier can actually send.
- **Headline:** How to turn a catering menu into a shopping list in whole packs
- **Snap:** You cannot ask the supplier for 2.4 cases.
- **Life:** The order deadline is close and the prep sheet is still written in cups.
- **Connection:** The buy list hands off to purchase orders and receiving.
