### Step 1 — The Idea

> A caterer who already runs Square and already pays for QuickBooks wants to know whether CostCook joins them or becomes a third island, and the answer is buried inside a feature group he will never open.

### Step 2 — Your Character

- **Want:** Know whether this ends the double entry between the front counter, the books and the kitchen.
- **Need:** A straight answer about what is not built, so he can judge the rest of the site by how this page behaves.
- **Wound:** Bought software on the strength of a roadmap item that never arrived.
- **Flaw:** Hears "in development" as "soon", and hears "built" as "available".

### Step 5 — Character Voices

- **Reader's words:** Square, QuickBooks, my bookkeeper, the till, double entry, does it connect, is it included.
- **Product voice:** candid, unhurried, specific about the difference between written and switched on.
- **Banned:** seamless, integrates with, coming soon on its own, any month or quarter, roadmap, "we are working hard on".

### Step 6 — Dialogue

- The heading: a hopeful question → a No that earns the next paragraph.
- Square: "being built" → written, complete, and switched off, which is not the same as available.
- QuickBooks: assumed to be beside Square → further back, a reserved name and nothing else.
- The API: a technical footnote → the honest answer to "can my current ordering tool talk to it".
- The closing: a lost sale → what the kitchen half does on its own, which is the actual offer.

### Step 7 — Sorkin

- **Intention:** Find out whether CostCook ends the double entry.
- **Obstacle:** Three connections at three different distances from working, any one of which could be rounded up into a promise.
- **Headline:** What CostCook connects to, and what it does not yet.
- **Subhead:** Square is written and switched off. QuickBooks is not started. Neither is in the app you would start today, and neither carries a date.

### Step 8 — Cool Talk

"Built is not the same as available, and this is the distance between them."

### Step 9 — Bringing a Scene to Life

- **Where they are:** At the counter after service, the Square terminal still lit, a folder of invoices for the bookkeeper beside it.
- **What they see:** The same numbers written down three times in three places.
- **Time of day:** The end of a long day, deciding whether to try one more piece of software.

### Step 10 — Connecting Your Scenes

- **POV:** You, locked throughout.
- **Hand-offs:** The FAQ's connections answer sends him here → the heading gives the No → the three cards separate the statuses → the closing hands him back to what CostCook does on its own → the crew feature area links here rather than answering it badly.

### Step 11 — Revise and Finish

- **Before → after:** One feature-group line item reading "Square and QuickBooks connections are being built" becomes three cards at three honestly different distances.
- **Claims removed:** That Square and QuickBooks are in the same state. That either one is being actively worked on now. Any implication of a date.
- **The ending is not a CTA.** It is the sentence about what CostCook does on its own, and it holds precisely because it asks for nothing. A page whose whole subject is three things that do not work yet is the worst place on the site to ask for a card.
- **Final image:** Three different answers, none of them softened, and a reason the rest is still worth having.
