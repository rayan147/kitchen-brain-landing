# Story Tracker — Supplier price changes

- **Idea:** A chef-owner receives a new supplier price, but cannot see which recipes and quotes still carry the old cost.
- **Character:** Wants the current number; needs source and effective date; remembers discovering the increase after quoting; updates only the spreadsheet in front of them.
- **Plot:** New sheet arrives → offers are normalized → current choice is reviewed → recipe costs move → open quotes are checked.
- **Scenes:** Keep the source; compare packs; choose deliberately; trace affected recipes; distinguish quoted and current cost; review the next order.
- **Voice:** Second person; evidence-led and restrained; no silent-write or guaranteed-margin claims.
- **Dialogue:** A changed case price stops being an isolated invoice line and becomes a visible decision path.
- **Headline:** How supplier price changes reach the recipes you already costed
- **Snap:** The risk starts when a draft quote still carries the old cost.
- **Life:** Friday's price list arrives after Monday's wedding quote was sent.
- **Connection:** Supplier changes hand off to invoice review and quoted-versus-current costing.
