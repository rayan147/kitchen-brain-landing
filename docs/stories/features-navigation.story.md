### Step 1 — The Idea

> A working chef-owner who wants to confirm that CostCook handles the next real kitchen job, but a complete feature inventory makes them translate software categories into their own week.

### Step 2 — Your Character

- **Want:** Find the relevant capability in one scan.
- **Need:** Recognize the job and arrive at evidence that CostCook handles it.
- **Wound:** A quote, delivery, or prep shift went wrong because the current number or plan was difficult to trust.
- **Flaw:** Scanning software terminology instead of starting with the kitchen problem.

### Step 5 — Character Voices

- **Reader's words:** recipe, food cost, menu, quote, supplier price, invoice, shop, prep, pack, receiving, shelf, month cost, invite, owner, manager, staff.
- **Product voice:** competent, calm, unfussy.
- **Banned:** seamless, powerful, optimize.

### Step 6 — Dialogue

- Features trigger: hidden depth → visible choice.
- Build and price: old or scattered inputs → a number ready to quote.
- Run the event: separate lists and receipts → one recognizable working path.
- Footer handoff: curated shortcuts → every shipped detail remains available.

### Step 7 — Sorkin

- **Intention:** Find whether CostCook handles the job in front of you.
- **Obstacle:** The complete list is too dense for global navigation.
- **Headline:** Build and price / Run the event.
- **Subhead:** Each destination names one outcome and lands on the matching feature section.

### Step 8 — Cool Talk

> Price a menu before you say the number out loud.

### Step 9 — Bringing a Scene to Life

- **Where:** A prep table or desk beside a menu or price list.
- **What:** A phone or laptop and little patience for translating software categories.
- **When:** The point in the week when a price, list, or plan needs an answer.

### Step 10 — Connecting Your Scenes

- **POV:** Second person throughout descriptions.
- **Hand-off:** Build and price → run the event → explore the complete shipped list.

### Step 11 — Revise and Finish

- **Word count before → after:** 139 words across eight draft descriptions → 103 words.
- **Claims removed:** No time-saved, revenue, customer, or automation claim added.
- **Final Image:** Choose the kitchen job or access question and land on the evidence for it.
