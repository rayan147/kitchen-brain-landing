### Step 1 — The Idea

> A kitchen owner wants one system for the regular week and special events, but four missing capabilities make the future shape of that plan unclear.

### Step 2 — Your Character

- **Want:** Know whether CostCook fits both recurring service and event work.
- **Need:** See what works today and what is genuinely being built without decoding a comparison grid.
- **Wound:** Bought software on the strength of a roadmap item that never arrived.
- **Flaw:** Reads “coming soon” as available now unless the boundary is stated beside it.

### Step 5 — Character Voices

- **Reader's words:** regular week, special event, label printing, dietary characteristics, Spanish, top back up to par, what is included today.
- **Product voice:** candid, kitchen-literate, specific.
- **Banned:** roadmap, soon-ish, seamless, available while a feature is marked Coming.

### Step 6 — Dialogue

- Two planning patterns: business category → actual shape of the week.
- Four missing jobs: absent capability → named future addition.
- Coming soon: optimistic promise → explicit boundary that it is not included today and has no promised date.
- Comparison link: summary → dated evidence.

### Step 7 — Sorkin

- **Intention:** See whether CostCook can grow into the rest of the kitchen week.
- **Obstacle:** A future feature can sound shipped unless its boundary sits beside it.
- **Headline:** Your regular week and your special events are two different plans.
- **Subhead:** Four additions are coming soon. None is included in the CostCook you would start today, and no date is promised.

### Step 8 — Cool Talk

The homepage keeps its established snap line—“The numbers move between them so you do not.”—and this section adds no rival.

### Step 9 — Bringing a Scene to Life

- **Where they are:** Looking at next week’s repeating service beside Saturday’s one-off event.
- **What they see:** A label roll, dietary notes, a Spanish-speaking teammate and an ingredient below par.
- **Time of day:** Planning the week before orders go out.

### Step 10 — Connecting Your Scenes

- **POV:** You, locked throughout.
- **Hand-offs:** Team access names who can work → the two-calendar figure separates the plans → four coming additions name the future edges → the dated comparison carries the evidence → founder trust follows.

### Step 11 — Revise and Finish

- **Before → after:** “Three are not planned” and one long label explanation become four parallel, visible Coming soon rows.
- **Claims removed:** English only as a permanent boundary; par buying as permanently absent; dietary characteristics as permanently unplanned.
- **Final image:** You see exactly four additions, each marked Coming soon, with “not included today” and “no date promised” in the same frame.
