Two documents fed this pass: `AUDIT-problem-clarity-2026-08-01.md` (page structure and
clarity) and `costcook-pitch.md` (claims checked against the app's code). They agree on

## The bug neither document caught, found while re-recording

**The page and the video were narrating two different menus as if they were one.**

`capture-silent.mjs` and `shot-product.mjs` both took *the first menu link*, which sorts to
**Mediterranean Mezze** ($4.36 a guest, $11.75 selling price). The order beside it is the
**Rodriguez backyard wedding**, which is built on **Summer BBQ**. So the page said "this
menu costs $4.36 a guest" and then, one shot later, "$1,354.04 of food for 200 guests" —
which is $6.77 a guest, off a different menu.

On a page whose entire claim is *they are all the same numbers*, that arithmetic not
closing is the section arguing against itself, and a caterer who divides $1,354 by 200
catches it in about four seconds.

Both rigs now pin **order, menu and recipe by name**, and every figure on the page and in
the video comes off one order:

| | |
|---|---|
| Order | Rodriguez backyard wedding, 200 guests, $16.50/guest, confirmed |
| Menu | Summer BBQ — $6.44 dish + $0.52 misc = **$6.96/guest**, 42.2%, charge ≥$23.20 |
| Order money | $3,300.00 revenue, $1,354.04 food, **$6.77/guest**, 41%, charge ≥$22.57 |
| Drift | quoted $6.77 → today $6.96, +2.8% |
| Vendor | Restaurant Depot, $354.30 |
| Recipe | Greek Salad (a Summer BBQ dish) — $1.60 + $0.13 = **$1.73**, sells $4.10, 42.2% |

$1,354.04 ÷ 200 = $6.77. It closes now.

## The video

Rebuilt end to end. **1:43**, seven beats, Coqui XTTS v2.

- **The script was the reason it sounded synthetic**, more than the engine. `beats.mjs`
  used to require the spoken line to be a transliteration of the on-screen caption, and
  captions are telegraphic by design ("The menu costs itself"). Read aloud that is not a
  sentence anybody says. Caption and narration now carry the same *facts* in different
  *words* — the accessibility requirement is content equivalence, not string equality.
- **There was no beginning.** It opened on a menu list with no indication of whose kitchen
  this is or what is at stake. `b0` now establishes the caterer, the wedding and the two
  weeks before anything is demonstrated.
- **Narration is synthesised per sentence**, not per beat, and rejoined with 0.28s of
  silence. XTTS's intonation flattens badly across a 35-word single shot; this was the
  largest engine-side gain available.
- Speed 0.94, and numbers spelled as spoken ("six ninety-six").
- The money summary and the quoted-vs-today band were two beats over an identical frame —
  half a minute with nothing moving. They are one beat now.
- `scrollTop` framing added, because `scrollIntoViewIfNeeded` is a no-op on an
  already-visible element and left the shopping-list beat framed on the Green Valley rows.

### The voice can be yours without you reading the script

XTTS clones from a short reference clip. Record ~20 seconds of *anything* and:

```
REF_WAV=scripts/demo-video/voices/owner.wav ENGINE=xtts \
  scripts/demo-video/venv/bin/python scripts/demo-video/speak.py
```

Every line then comes out in your timbre, and it survives script changes. **The CPML
position is unchanged** — XTTS weights are non-commercial and Coqui is gone — so the only
fully clean option is still you reading the seven lines and dropping them in as
`vo-silent/bN.wav`, which the rig picks up with no code change.

## The one disagreement, and how it was resolved

Both documents wanted the `h1` changed. They wanted different lines.

- **The audit** recommended *"The client moves 180 to 240 on Thursday. Do the math once."*
- **The pitch doc** said the workload framing is the symptom, and that *"the money is
  decided on the phone, months before anybody cooks"* is the sharper claim and is missing
  from the page entirely.

**The pitch doc wins, in the audit's form.** The recount is the time cost; the quote call
is the money, and the page already spends its whole problem section on arithmetic. So the
headline is the pitch doc's insight, written as the audit asked — a specific, falsifiable
bad moment in the largest type:

> **The money is decided on the phone, months before anybody cooks.**
>
> Somebody asks what you would do 180 head for, and you say a number that sounds about
> right. Type in the menu and the guest count instead…

This is a judgment call on the most consequential line on the page. Overrule it if you
disagree; the audit's version is one edit away.

## Second pass — the loop the page was not showing

Checked every claim against `develop` before writing any of it. **All of it is shipped.**
Two things the page had wrong in opposite directions:

- **`priceSheet` intake accepts photos.** `import/+page.server.ts` takes JPEG/PNG/WebP,
  PDF and CSV. The PDF-only rule is `ImportWorkspace.svelte:210` and applies to
  `sourceType === 'invoice'` **alone**. So "photograph the price board" is true, "snap the
  invoice" was false, and my earlier fix — "send the invoice as a PDF" — was correct but
  undersold the thing a caterer would actually do. Stage 01 and `WhatWeDo` 05 now say it.
- **The chain had a hole in the middle.** It went shopping list → "check the truck off",
  skipping the two steps that connect them, and never said where a purchase lands.

`TheLoop` is now nine stages, headed **"Price sheet in, PO out, invoice in."**
New: **06 PO OUT** (`emailPurchaseOrder` — recomposed server-side, frozen only after the
transport accepts), **07 RECEIVING** rewritten around `/purchases/receiving/[poId]` — the
sent PO *is* the sheet and the tick writes the purchase — and **08 ON HAND**, which is why
the loop closes at all.

Ten shipped capabilities that are not links in the chain went into a compact **"Also in
there"** list rather than ten more cards. That is a deliberate tension with the audit,
which rates this the longest section on the page: coverage went up, height barely moved.

Two of those — quoted-vs-today and event cost attribution — were previously excluded by a
comment in this file saying they need weeks of data before they show anything. That was
already inconsistent, since quoted-vs-today is in the video and in the proof shots.

**One thing I did not write as given.** The offline stamp was quoted as *"cached at
4:52am"*. The app actually says *"Offline — showing this page as loaded 4:52 AM. Reconnect
to refresh."* Quoting UI copy the product does not say is the same class of error as a
caption disagreeing with its screenshot, so the line describes the behaviour instead.

**Your message was cut off** at "a one-click sample kitchen at" — I wrote it as the
capability (`routes/demo/+server.ts`, one-tap sign-in to a seeded kitchen) without a URL.
If you meant a specific address on the page, say which.

## Third pass — screenshots for the back half of the loop

`ProofShots` covers the wedge (one order, price to pan). Everything after the shopping list
was claimed in prose and never shown. New `LoopShots.astro`, seven screens, captured by
`e2e/.scratch/shot-loop.mjs` off real seeded rows:

| Shot | What it proves |
|---|---|
| 01 PRICE IN | The dropzone's own words: *"PDF, photo, CSV, or DOCX"* |
| 04 WHOLE POTS | Lemon-Garlic Marinade, 2 L per batch, *make 5 of 5* |
| 06 PO OUT | Two suppliers, both emailed Sat 1 Aug, needed by Mon 3 Aug; 6 of 7 received |
| 07 RECEIVING | The sent PO as the check-off sheet; one line open, hint reads *now $31.40/pack* |
| 08 THE PURCHASE | Ledger: the tick wrote $421.15 / 14 items, beside a July *Invoice import* row |
| 08 ON HAND | $507.80 on the shelf — and *20 need a count*, stated rather than hidden |
| 09 THE MONTH | $1,447.70 should-have-cost vs $421.15 spent, gap named as food on the shelf |

**Read-only by contract.** The capture never submits a form and deliberately never opens
the "Prepare purchase orders" composer, because that dialog can send a real email. The
sent-orders table proves the same claim from rows that already exist.

**Two orders, and the section says so.** The Rodriguez wedding is two weeks out and has not
been ordered; the Fairview lunch is in three days and is already received. So the later
screens are Fairview's. Dressing one order up as simultaneously unordered and received was
the only alternative, and it would have been a lie.

**One screen that could not be captured honestly.** The diff-review step — *tap what
changed* — needs a staged import batch, and the walkthrough seed creates none. Rather than
mock one, 01 shows the door it comes in through. Seed an import batch and it can be real.

**Layout.** Wide crops take `width: 100%`, `max-width: <1:1>`, `min-width: 720px`. On a
desktop column they land just under 1:1 and are fully visible; on a phone the floor forces
overflow inside an `overflow-x-auto` wrapper, so a reader swipes instead of squinting at 29
percent. Two earlier versions were wrong: a bare 1:1 width clipped the right-hand column off
every table at 1440, and `max-width: 100%` alone shrank the image back to its scroll
container and left the wrapper with nothing to scroll.

## Left undone, deliberately

**Structural cuts (audit §7) — your call, not a fix.** The audit rates these Clarify, with
stated trade-offs, and they change what the page argues rather than correcting it:

- Cut `HOW FAR IT GOES` on mobile (8 `h3`s across 2,000px for features the section's own
  lede says you will not touch in week one). Biggest scroll saving available.
- Cut `WHAT IT LOOKS LIKE` from five shots to two. It is ~28% of page height.
- Move `THE PROBLEM` closer to the fold.

**App-repo work, out of scope for a landing pass:**

- **The phone menu header collision.** The selling-price input's `$ 16.50` and `per guest`
  paint over the sticky header's wordmark at 390px. Real bug; the capture now avoids the
  band rather than fixing it.
- **Phone shop rows.** The stepper takes ~310px of 390, so ingredient text wraps to four
  or five lines, and the decrement button sits beside an em-dash value — two adjacent
  minus signs. Visible in `shop-mobile.png`.
- **Decimal counts on countable items** ("304.9 each" bell pepper). Whether counted units
  should round in the shopping list is a domain decision in a system whose premise is
  exact quantities. Flagged, not prescribed.
- **"Shop list" vs "shopping list".** The page now says shopping list everywhere; the app's
  header says "Shop list". Renaming a control is a stealth E2E break (specs locate by
  accessible name), so it needs its own commit with the spec updates.

**Buyer questions the page still does not answer** (pitch doc §7): what it will cost later,
what happens to the data if you stop, and — stated as a headline rather than in passing —
that it works on a phone in the walk-in.

**One pacing call I made and could be wrong about.** The merged order beat holds 26.4s on a
static frame — a quarter of the video. Narration runs continuously across it and the screen
is dense, so it should read as unhurried rather than stalled, but I cannot judge that from
stills. The obvious fix, a mid-beat scroll, is the one I rejected: scrolling down from the
money summary lands on the Green Valley rows and puts "Bell pepper — need 304.9 each" on
camera for thirteen seconds, which is a concrete credibility cost against an uncertain
pacing gain. Watch 40s–66s; if it drags, the cheap fix is trimming a sentence, not scrolling.
