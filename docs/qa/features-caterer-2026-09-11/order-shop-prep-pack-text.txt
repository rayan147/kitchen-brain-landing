Frequently asked questions

The practical questions behind taking one confirmed event from the first buy line to the last item in the van.

Do Shop, Prep, and Pack use the same order?

Yes. All three read the computed plan for the event. Confirming the order freezes the plan, math, and money together so the working day keeps the commitment you made.

Can the shopping list use what is already on hand?

Each buy line can show need, trusted on hand, pack size, and packs. Order-scoped shelf checks keep the decision attached to this event rather than changing a shared number behind another plan.

What happens when the plan changes?

Checks stay saved to the order. A plan change clears only the checks it made stale, so completed work is not erased without reason and outdated work does not stay checked.

Can the crew print the lists?

Yes. Shop, Prep, and Pack print without controls, while useful hints remain and columns align for a clipboard.

What does Pack carry besides food?

Pack includes per-order equipment, lets you hide a template line or add a one-off, and carries allergen badges for the dishes leaving the kitchen.
