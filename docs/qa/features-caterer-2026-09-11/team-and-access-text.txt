What this is not.

CostCook does not let you hide one screen, expose one field, or assemble a custom role. If a cook must receive a prep list without being able to open costs, that boundary is not available today.

If hiding costs is essential, CostCook is not the right fit today. If you want to check your setup before deciding, book a 15-min demo.

Questions to settle first.

Check cost visibility, invitations and role limits before adding your team.

Can I hide food costs from a cook?

No. CostCook does not have per-screen permissions, so a workspace teammate can open cost screens. The role boundaries protect specific sensitive actions, not every view.

Can I build a custom role?

No. The available roles are Owner, Manager and Staff. There is no permission builder for individual screens, fields or actions.

What role does a new invitation receive?

Invited teammates join as Staff today. Invitations are not a self-service path for building a custom manager role.

Are restricted actions actually blocked?

No. CostCook checks your role when you try a restricted action, even if you follow a direct link.
