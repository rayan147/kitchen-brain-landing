ORDERS, THE EVENT

Quote it, freeze it, run it.
Costed before commitment. A new order is a menu, a date, guests, and a price, estimated live as you set it up.
Three tabs, one plan. Shop, Prep, and Pack all read the same computed plan.
Pinned money bar. The numbers stay on screen while you work, and guest-count changes autosave quietly.
Confirm freezes everything. Confirmation saves the plan, calculations and quoted price together. Later price changes do not rewrite that quote.
Honest reopen. Reopening keeps the frozen prices and says so. Once purchasing starts, reopening is refused.
Clean duplication. Copy an event into one draft. A double-tap cannot make two.
Shelf check. Enter the ingredients and prepared batches you have on hand for this order.
Buy math shown. Every buy line can show need, on hand, pack size, and packs.
Vendor grouping. The shopping list groups by supplier and by kitchen section.
Durable check-offs. Checked items stay saved. If the plan changes, affected items need checking again.
Walk-in mode. On a phone, a running left-to-grab footer follows you through the list.
Printable lists. Shop, prep, and pack print clean: controls gone, hints kept, columns aligned for a clipboard.
Leftover nudges. Likely left about a quart, from your recent orders. One at a time, dismissible.
Quoted vs today. Confirmed orders compare the frozen quote with current prices, and say when they cannot.
Event cost, after. Compare the confirmed quote with purchases recorded against the event. Differences below the configured dollar threshold are not highlighted.
Shortfall evidence. Pack lines left open at close become reviewable evidence: short, packed, or not sure.
Aged plans flagged. An old frozen plan is marked old. It is never silently recomputed.
Per-order equipment. Hide a template line or add a one-off for this event. The menu template never changes.
Allergens at pack-out. Pack lists carry allergen badges.
Orders hub. Filter, search, and read food-cost and status badges across every event.
Frozen prep notes. Draft orders use the current prep notes. Confirmed orders keep the notes saved when you confirmed.

PURCHASE ORDERS & RECEIVING

What you send, and what actually arrives.
Per-vendor POs. Confirming an order offers purchase orders per supplier: email, print, or handled by you.
The email you saw. Review the exact supplier email before sending. Check the order record for whether it was sent or needs a retry.
Printable POs. Signature lines and stable PO numbers.
Receiving follows sending. A sent PO creates its receiving checklist for you.
Deliveries as they are. Short, over, substituted, missing, and unexpected lines each have their own handling.
All-or-nothing posting. Committing a delivery writes real purchases, and current prices update from what actually arrived.
Automatic rebuy list. Short and missing lines become a durable follow-up list with handled and reopen states.
Receive from the purchase order. Receive against any sent purchase order from its own screen.
Retryable sends. A failed PO send can be retried, with the failure kept on record.

INVENTORY

The shelf, computed, never guessed.
Stock on hand. Start with a dated physical count, then add deliveries and subtract recorded use and waste.
Dated count history. Each count keeps the unit cost recorded at the time. A later correction does not replace the old count.
Count freshness. See whether stock is Fresh, Stale or Never counted. Missing or old counts do not reduce estimated buying.
Desktop and phone views. A desktop table and a phone list, with state badges and a need breakdown naming which order wants what.
Buy what is missing. Subtract trusted stock from order needs to build a printable shopping list. Quantities you override stay separate.
Shelf value. What the shelf was worth at any date.
