Three answers, and there is no fourth.

Every dish on the order gets one, and the line names the ingredient that caused it.

Conflict

A reviewed ingredient contains the named allergen or has a characteristic that conflicts with the guest’s diet.

Check

Review is needed: an ingredient may contain the allergen, information is missing, or the diet requires checks the app cannot make.

Clear

Every ingredient has been reviewed and no conflict was found in the recorded information. This is not a guarantee about the food you serve.

The worst answer across the guests is the one the row carries, so a dish is never quieter than its most serious reason. The same reading prints on the order, the prep list and the pack list.

Questions to settle first.

The ones a chef asks before trusting any of it.

Does a clear result mean the dish is fine to serve?

It means every ingredient in the dish was reviewed and none of them says otherwise. It is a reason to serve, not a promise about a guest. No screen in CostCook makes an allergen-free claim.

Why can halal and kosher only ever come back as a check?

Because the facts that decide them are not in a recipe. Halal meat depends on the slaughter and kosher meat on certification, and no ingredient row can carry either. CostCook checks what it can see, forbids pork and alcohol for halal and pork and shellfish for kosher, flags meat with dairy on a kosher order, and stops there.

What happens to dishes with ingredients nobody has reviewed?

They come back as a check, never as clear, and the order tells you how many ingredients are outstanding. The count is on the order once, not repeated on every dish, so it reads as a job to finish rather than noise.

Does the answer update if I change a recipe after confirming?

No, and the screen says so. Confirming an order freezes the reading it was checked on and carries the date it was checked. A later edit to a recipe does not silently restate it. Re-check reads the catalog again and appends a new reading beside the old one.

Do I have to tag every ingredient before this is useful?

Setup drafts the rows from your ingredient names so the repetitive part is not yours. Every draft stays editable, uncertainty stays as do not know, and only your confirmation becomes a fact the guards will use.
