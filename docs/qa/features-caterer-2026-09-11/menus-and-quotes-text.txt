Frequently asked questions

The practical questions to settle before the number leaves your kitchen.

What is menu management software?

Menu management software keeps dishes, portions per guest, the default selling price, and reusable equipment counts together. In CostCook, the menu can then become the starting point for a costed event.

How does CostCook calculate a catering quote?

A draft combines a menu, date, guest count, and price. CostCook computes the food cost per guest and food-cost percentage, then shows the price that would meet the configured target before you confirm.

Can an event use a different price from the menu default?

Yes. The menu carries the default per-guest price, and an individual order can override it without changing the reusable menu.

What happens when I confirm a quote?

Confirming saves the event plan, calculations and quoted price together. Later ingredient prices do not rewrite the quote you gave.

Will later ingredient prices change the quote I already gave?

No. A confirmed event keeps its frozen prices. CostCook can compare the original quote with current prices later without silently rewriting the commitment.

Can I reopen a confirmed event?

You can reopen while preserving the frozen prices, and the screen says that clearly. Once purchasing has started, reopening is refused so completed work is not invalidated.
