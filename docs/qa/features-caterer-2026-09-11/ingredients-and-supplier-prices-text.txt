Frequently asked questions

The practical questions behind comparing a supplier price you can trust.

Can one ingredient have prices from more than one supplier?

Yes. Concurrent supplier offers sit on the same ingredient and compare per usable unit, cheapest first. You choose which offer becomes the current price.

How does CostCook compare different pack sizes?

Each offer keeps its pack quantity, purchase unit, price, and usable yield. CostCook converts those facts to the same usable-unit cost so a case and a bag can be compared on equal terms.

Where did the current price come from?

Every current price carries its source, supplier, and effective date. Purchase history remains underneath the ingredient instead of being replaced by one unexplained number.

What happens when a supplier price changes?

Before you adopt the change, CostCook shows the recipes and menus that use the ingredient. Once applied, recipes using it recost from the new ingredient price.

What if a price or conversion is missing?

The catalog names ingredients that still need a fact. Recipe totals that cannot be fully costed stay marked partial rather than quietly treating the missing line as free.

Can I bring in existing ingredient data?

Yes. Import ingredients from a CSV spreadsheet, or upload supplier paperwork. Review and confirm the suggested details before they change costs.
