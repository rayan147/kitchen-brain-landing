SIGN-IN & RELIABILITY

Built for wet hands and thin signal.
No passwords. Sign-in is an invitation and a magic link. Links work once and die in eight minutes.
Access ends now. Remove someone and their access stops on their next request, not their next sign-in.
Safe return links. Sign-in destinations are checked, so a crafted link cannot bounce you off-site.
Read saved pages without signal. Previously opened order pages can be read without signal and show when they were saved. Reconnect to make changes.
Works without JavaScript. Core pages remain readable if browser scripts are unavailable.
Readable text and touch controls. Text enlargement, contrast and touch targets are checked in interface tests.
Notices that behave. Important messages remain visible when an action takes you to another page.
Service status. The service status check does not repeatedly reload kitchen records.

SAGE, THE IN-APP ASSISTANT

Sage, answering from your own numbers.
Ask during setup. Setup keeps an Ask Sage entry, offers questions that fit the stage and records entered so far, and gives you a direct route back.
Eleven checks, one proposal. Ask about the shift, recipes, stock, buying or setup. Sage can also prepare a shopping-list proposal for a manager or owner to approve.
Sources under the answer. Each answer shows the records and checks behind its numbers, and says when evidence is missing.
A proposal is not a change. Answers read your records. A shopping-list draft waits for an Owner or Manager to approve it, with the records checked again before saving.

WHAT IT CONNECTS TO

Your supplier does not have to be plugged in. CostCook reads the paper.

The reason there is no long list of connectors here is that prices get in without one. An order guide, a price sheet, an invoice, or a photograph of a delivery slip taken on the loading dock all go through the same queue.

Five doors in

A photo, a PDF, a spreadsheet, a Word document, or text you paste. Every one lands in the same review.

Review before saving

What comes out sits outside your catalog until you confirm it. Nothing reaches a plate cost that you have not seen.

What it could not read

Anything unreadable is quoted back to you word for word, beside the suggested details, so you can check it against the original.
