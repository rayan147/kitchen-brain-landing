Your lead cook has been with you three years, fast and careful. You want her in the system: building the prep list, checking in Thursday's delivery, fixing the recipe when the new pan holds less.

You don't want her doing the math on the wedding. $9,600 for 120 guests, and she knows what the chicken cost because she unpacked it.

Most owners solve this by keeping the cooks out of the software altogether. Then the prep list lives on a whiteboard, and the whiteboard is wrong.

## What a cook needs

Go through a prep day and write down what your cook looks at:

- **How much.** Forty pounds of chicken thigh, four quarts of dressing.
- **For what.** Which event, which day, which dish.
- **What's in it.** The recipe, the method, the allergens.
- **What came in.** Four 10-lb cases on the shelf, or three and a short.

There's no dollar sign on that list. A cook needs to know it's four cases. Not that it's $219.

## Three roles, and where the line falls

CostCook has three roles, and the line between them is costs.

- **Owner.** Everything, and the only one who manages the team, settings and billing.
- **Manager.** Everything Staff can do, plus costs, vendors and purchase orders. No billing.
- **Staff.** Works from orders, prep lists, deliveries and recipes. Can't change prices, settings or billing.

There's no grid of custom permissions to fiddle with. If someone should see costs, they're a Manager. If they shouldn't, they're Staff.

## What Staff see instead of a price

Prices, costs and margins are taken out on the server before the page reaches a Staff login, so there's no column to switch back on. The quantities and units stay.

So your cook opens the order and sees forty pounds of chicken thigh, the prep list, the pack list. She opens the recipe and can fix the method or the yield. She can't change a price, on an ingredient, a recipe or an order.

Some screens are all money. Analytics, Sage, the Clients book, events and proposals, and the price and spending pages under Purchases are closed to Staff, and Analytics, Events and Clients drop out of the menu. Follow an old link to one and you get a plain page: "The page is fine. Nothing broke and nothing was changed: this screen needs a different kitchen role." And a button back to Today.

Receiving is shared. Staff can record what came off the truck, by quantity. Committing the delivery and logging what was paid takes a Manager or you.

## Invite them the right way

Only the Owner invites. In **Settings > Team & access**, under **Invite people**, type their emails and choose **They join as**: **Staff** or **Manager**. They get a one-time sign-in link by email. No password to remember.

The link works for 7 days, and you can change someone's role later. If a cook becomes your sous and should start seeing food cost, that's **Change role**, not a new account.

When someone leaves, **Remove from kitchen** signs them out on every device right away. The orders, counts and notes they made stay, with their name on them.

## Who gets Manager

Hand out Manager the way you'd hand out the office key: to whoever orders when you're not there, or prices a quote while you're on a job. And only to someone you'd trust to read the month's food cost and keep it to themselves.

Everyone else is Staff, with everything they need to cook from.

## Check the invites

- Your cooks are Staff: quantities, recipes and lists, never prices.
- Your managers can open costs, vendors and purchase orders, and you're fine with that.
- You know where **Change role** and **Remove from kitchen** are.

Thursday your lead cook checks in the delivery from her phone, four cases of thigh, and the price of chicken stays your business.
